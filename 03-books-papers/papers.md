## How to read them fast
- First pass: abstract, intro, architecture figure, conclusion (about 20 min each).
- Do a deep read only of GFS, MapReduce, Dynamo, Dremel, Spark RDD and Lakehouse. These come up most in DE interviews and system-design rounds.
- Pair Dremel with the Parquet docs, Kafka with "The Log", and Delta Lake with Lakehouse.
