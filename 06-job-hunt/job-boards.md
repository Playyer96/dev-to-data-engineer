## 5. Boolean search strings

LinkedIn supports `AND`, `OR`, `NOT` (UPPERCASE), `"exact phrase"` and `( )`. Reference: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a524335 (OK).
Put the string in the **Keywords** box. Then set **Remote** in the "On-site/Remote" filter, set **Date posted = Past 24 hours**, and set Location to `Latin America`, `Colombia`, or `United States` (try all three: many US-posted remote roles accept LATAM contractors).

URL tricks for LinkedIn job search links (append to the search URL):
- `&f_WT=2`: remote only
- `&f_TPR=r86400`: posted in the last 24h (`r604800` = last week)
- `&sortBy=DD`: newest first

### LinkedIn: core Data Engineer
```
("data engineer" OR "analytics engineer" OR "data platform engineer" OR "ETL developer") AND (SQL) AND (AWS OR Python OR dbt) NOT (principal OR staff OR "director")
```

### LinkedIn: explicitly LATAM-friendly
```
("data engineer" OR "analytics engineer" OR "data analyst") AND (LATAM OR "Latin America" OR "South America" OR Colombia OR "nearshore" OR "Americas")
```

### LinkedIn: analyst-leaning (fastest to land)
```
("data analyst" OR "product analyst" OR "analytics engineer" OR "BI developer") AND (SQL) AND (PostgreSQL OR dbt OR Python OR Looker OR Tableau OR "Power BI")
```

### LinkedIn: bridge roles (backend + data, easier to get hired today)
```
("software engineer" OR "backend engineer") AND ("data pipeline" OR ETL OR "event data" OR ingestion OR "data platform") AND (AWS OR Lambda OR serverless)
```

### LinkedIn: domain advantage (gaming / sports-tech / telemetry)
```
("data engineer" OR "data analyst" OR "analytics engineer") AND (gaming OR games OR "game analytics" OR telemetry OR "sports tech" OR simulation OR IoT OR sensor)
```

### LinkedIn POSTS search (hidden jobs: "we're hiring" posts). Use the Posts filter, not Jobs
```
("hiring" OR "we're hiring" OR "looking for") AND ("data engineer" OR "analytics engineer") AND (remote) AND (LATAM OR "Latin America" OR Colombia)
```

## 6. Reading a listing for LATAM eligibility (save time)
- **Green flags:** "LATAM", "Americas", "anywhere", "EST/CST overlap", "contractor", "paid in USD", "Deel/Remote.com/Oyster" (EOR providers, e.g. https://www.deel.com/, OK).
- **Red flags:** "must be authorized to work in the US", "US-based only", "W-2 only", "security clearance", "remote within the US". Skip these unless a recruiter tells you otherwise.
- **Ambiguous "Remote":** apply anyway, and say clearly in the first line of your note that you're in Colombia working US hours, contractor-ready.
